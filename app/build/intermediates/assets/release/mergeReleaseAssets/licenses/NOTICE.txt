TvSama

TvSama is a separate application with its own interface and Android package.
It incorporates the Streamflix Reborn library, distributed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0 (see streamflix-Apache-2.0.txt).

Original Streamflix: Copyright 2022 Lory-Stan TANASI.
Streamflix Reborn: Copyright 2025 Streamflix Reborn contributors.
Upstream: https://github.com/streamflix-reborn2/streamflix

The Streamflix source distributed in this project was adapted for use as an
Android library. TvSama supplies its own navigation, presentation and playback
integration. The original source and license remain in the streamflix folder.
